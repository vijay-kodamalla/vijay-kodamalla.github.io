SGLang — NVFP4 W4A16 GEMM on Blackwell SM120

CUDA · FlashInfer · CUTLASS · CUPTI · Aug 2026

Assigned an open issue: decide whether FlashInfer's bf16×fp4 kernel should replace the incumbent Marlin path for dense NVFP4 W4A16 layers on SM120. Benchmarked both across 21 configurations on an RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell — three Qwen3.6-27B GEMM shapes, decode batch sizes 1–128, CUPTI timing with CUDA graphs and cold L2, one shared quantization so both kernels read byte-identical weights. Marlin won 15 of 21; FlashInfer's cute-dsl backend was ~23% more accurate in all of them. Both plateau at 57–64% of the card's 1597 GB/s on the MLP shapes and 82% on the output projection — the shape moves that number more than the kernel choice does. The work also established that the Marlin path is numerically correct on SM120, which CI had never asserted; that fix is merged.
